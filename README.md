## ✨ 100% Visual / Zero-Text Kid-Friendly Design

- **✂️ Swipe or Tap to Cut Ropes**: Slice the golden ropes holding the treats (`🍭 🍩 🧁 🎂 🍦 🍪 🧇 🍬`), collect all 3 golden stars (`⭐ × 3`), and feed the hungry Smiling Critters!
- **🫧 Floating Bubbles, 🍄 Bouncy Mushrooms, 🎈 Air Puffers & ⭕ Rope Rings**: Pure visual toy physics gadgets with an animated pointing finger (`👆✂️`) guide and a kid-proof Rainbow Cloud Cushion at the bottom so the candy is never lost.
- **🐾 8 Smiling Critter Stages**: DogDay, Hoppy Hopscotch, Bobby BearHug, PickyPiggy, Bubba Bubbaphant, CraftyCorn, KickinChicken, and CatNap.
